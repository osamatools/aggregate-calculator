How is the university aggregate calculated?

Each part is converted to a percentage and then multiplied by the weight your university gives it:

Aggregate = (Matric % × Matric weight) + (Inter % × Inter weight) + (Test % × Test weight)

Example: with 980/1100 in Matric (weight 10%), 950/1100 in Inter (weight 40%) and 250/400 in ECAT (weight 50%), the aggregate is 8.91 + 34.55 + 31.25 ≈ 74.70%.

How is the Honhaar Scholarship aggregate calculated?

Aggregate = (Matric % × 30%) + (Inter % × 70%). For example, 980/1100 in Matric and 950/1100 in Inter gives 26.73 + 60.45 = 87.18%.
