How is semester GPA calculated?

Multiply each course's grade points by its credit hours, add them up, then divide by the total credit hours:

GPA = Σ (grade points × credit hours) ÷ Σ credit hours

Example: a 3-credit course with A (4.00), a 3-credit course with B+ (3.33) and a 1-credit lab with A- (3.67) give (12 + 9.99 + 3.67) ÷ 7 = 3.67.

How is CGPA calculated?

New CGPA = (old CGPA × old credit hours + this semester's quality points) ÷ total credit hours. Example: a CGPA of 3.20 over 36 credit hours plus a 3.67 GPA over 18 credit hours gives (115.2 + 66.06) ÷ 54 = 3.36.

Is this calculator official?

No. It is a free helper tool. Your official GPA and CGPA are the ones on your university's result card or transcript.
